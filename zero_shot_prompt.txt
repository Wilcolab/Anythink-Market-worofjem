Zero-Shot Prompt Template
=========================

A zero-shot prompt is a request given to an AI without any prior examples or context.
It relies on the AI's pre-trained knowledge to understand and respond appropriately.

Example Format:
---------------

Task: [Description of what you want the AI to do]

Additional Instructions:
- [Any specific requirements]
- [Format preferences]
- [Tools or frameworks to use]

Zero-Shot Prompt Best Practices:
1. Be specific and clear in your request
2. Include all necessary context
3. Define the expected output format
4. Mention any constraints or limitations
5. Ask for explanation if needed

Benefits:
- No examples needed
- Faster to set up
- Good for well-known tasks
- Relies on general knowledge

Limitations:
- May need iteration
- Less precise than few-shot
- Requires clear instructions
- May struggle with novel tasks
